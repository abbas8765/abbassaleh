A 6.0 m × 3.0 m steel maintenance platform (W310 beams, W150 joists, HSS posts, L-angle X-bracing), checked for gravity loads by three independent methods: hand calculation in Mathcad, a parametric Excel workbook and a 3D Robot model. Drawn in AutoCAD and modelled in Revit, with a material take-off and a review register. Issued for review to S. Azoubaidy, P.Eng.; revision R1 (tension-only bracing and guard lateral loads) in progress. Student exercise: elastic analysis only, not a CSA S16 design.

Key highlights

  • Beam and joist results agreed within 0.1% across Mathcad, Excel and Robot.
  • Traced a 1.7% post-load difference to bracing self-weight, and found the X-bracing carrying about 4.9 kN of gravity load.
  • Found the long braces over the slenderness limit (L/r ≈ 537 vs 300), so specified a bolted crossing and tension-only bracing.
  • Caught a modelling error from the Revit schedule (braces modelled as W-sections) and corrected it. The take-off matched the Revit volumes within 2.3%.
